The architecture relies on a unique three-step pipeline seamlessly powered by the Alpaca API and StockTwits:

**1. The "Eyes": StockTwits Social Sentiment Engine**
Nightwatcher constantly scans StockTwits 24/7 to identify newly trending tickers and calculates localized bullish/bearish sentiment scores and message volumes in real-time. 

**2. The "Brain": LLM Catalyst Validation Layer**
Retail hype is famously a lagging indicator. To protect against pump-and-dump meme spikes, Nightwatcher automatically intercepts every StockTwits trend and forces it through a strict LLM Validation Layer. The agent instantly pulls down real-time market data and the latest news headlines via Alpaca’s data streams, and asks the LLM to classify the trend. If it's pure "Retail Noise," the bot drops it. If it's a verified "Hard Catalyst" (e.g., an earnings blowout or FDA approval), the signal passes. 

**3. The "Hands": Alpaca’s Institutional-Grade Execution**
This is where Alpaca shines as the absolute backbone of the system. The exact millisecond the LLM validates the catalyst, Nightwatcher fires a programmatic market order directly into the Alpaca execution layer with zero manual friction. 

By deeply integrating with your ecosystem, we unlocked massive capabilities for the agent:
* **Unified Equities & 24/7 Crypto:** Because Alpaca supports both asset classes, Nightwatcher seamlessly pivots between traditional equities during market hours and continues to hunt for crypto momentum overnight and across the weekend. 
* **Real-Time Portfolio Sync:** Alpaca acts as the direct source of truth for Nightwatcher's dashboard. Cash balances, buying power, real-time P&L, and historical portfolio snapshots are all kept in perfect sync. 
* **Programmatic Risk Validation:** Before submitting any order, Nightwatcher validates the trade against our Alpaca account status, programmatically enforcing strict maximum position sizes and tight stop-loss thresholds at the broker level.
