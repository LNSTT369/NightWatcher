# Nightwatcher V2: The AI Trading Agent with an Analyst's Intuition

Nightwatcher is a fully autonomous, multi-agent trading system that marries the speed of algorithms with the logic of a fundamental analyst. While other bots blindly chase retail hype and lagging sentiment on platforms like StockTwits, Nightwatcher deploys an advanced LLM Catalyst Validation Layer. The moment a ticker starts trending, it instantly cross-references the hype against real-time fundamental market data and breaking news, filtering out the "noise" of meme pumps to uncover verified, high-conviction catalysts. Crucially, this intelligence is directly wired into the market via deep integration with the Alpaca API, enabling Nightwatcher to execute these high-conviction trades instantly, twenty-four hours a day across both equities and crypto with institutional-grade speed and zero manual friction. It’s not just algorithmic trading—it’s intelligent, end-to-end automated alpha generation.
